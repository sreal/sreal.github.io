Selected work

The reach and frequency engine

Problem
The company's flagship analytics needed to say how many people an ad reached across TV networks, and how often, counted once each. The raw data was impression logs, with bot traffic mixed in.
What I did
Designed and built the engine in the data warehouse. It works out de-duplicated reach and frequency from the raw impressions and filters out bot traffic by how much each source sends in a week.
Result
The flagship cross-media analytics run on it. I have maintained it across nearly ten releases.

Client reports without the manual build

Problem
Each client's Tableau reports were built by hand, one client at a time.
What I did
Built a generator that produces each client's workbooks from templates. I wrote nearly all of it over about two years.
Result
The manual build was replaced, and the team now owns and extends the generator.

The capture rig

Problem
Measuring streaming TV means recording what is on screen, around the clock, without someone watching each device.
What I did
Built the rig and the software that drives it: it checks what each device is showing, decides what to do next, and can be controlled remotely from Slack.
Result
Grew it to about 40 streaming devices, then handed it to the team, who run it today.
